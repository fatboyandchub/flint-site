Questions, answered.

How is Flint different from Mail or Gmail?

Traditional apps show email the way servers store it: threads full of quoted history, subject lines, and signatures. Flint reads the thread for you and shows just what each person actually said, as chat bubbles, grouped by sender, newest at the bottom. It feels like Messages, but every message is real email.

Which accounts can I connect?

Gmail and Google Workspace, Outlook and Microsoft 365, iCloud Mail, Yahoo, AOL, and any standard IMAP mailbox. You can connect several at once and switch instantly.

Where is my email stored?

On your iPhone, in an encrypted local cache. That's also why search and scrolling are instant. Flint talks directly to your mail provider; there is no Flint cloud in between, and your credentials never leave the iOS Keychain.

Do my replies look weird to other people?

No. What you send is a normal email, from your normal address, with proper reply threading. Recipients can't tell you sent it from Flint.

When can I get it?

Flint is in final testing and coming soon to the App Store for iPhone. It will be free to download at launch.
